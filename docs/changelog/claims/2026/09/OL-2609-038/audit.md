### Findings
- **F1** test narrower than claim - `ProductInstallTest.php @1f2e2b25` never exercises `requires_bot: false`, so the `product.bot_on` NOT_APPLICABLE arm of C11 that C13 says it covers is unasserted (`WiringFacts.php:367 @1f2e2b25`); add a manifest with `requires_bot` false or drop C11 from C13's list.
- **F2** test narrower than claim - `ProductChatInstallsTest.php:103-122 @1f2e2b25`: the chain-alias and builtin-name refusals assert only the throw, not "zero `recipe_instances` rows", and `fbdraw`'s `permission_level` (:65) is never read; C32's "each" and "both" overstate what the file asserts.
- **F3** claim contradicted in-file - C23's `product.command` sentence ("NOT_APPLICABLE for a manifest without the matching installs section") is false of `WiringFacts.php:407-416 @1f2e2b25`, where the wire stays applicable while any of the three chat sections is present; C31 in the same claim states the shipped rule, so strike the command half of C23.
- **F4** mistagged, checkable as [code] - the second clause of C15 (`.prettierignore` excludes `resources/recipes/**/*.md`) and of C26 (`lane.md` sets no foreach cap) are in-repo facts filed under [unverified]; both are true (`.prettierignore:25 @HEAD`, `lane.md:51 @1f2e2b25`) but should be split out as [code].
- **F5** scope - `routes/web.php:694 @1f2e2b25` puts `products.install` behind `throttle:kit-fork`, sharing the kit-fork rate limiter; no claim, Surface line or Unchanged line mentions the throttle. OL-2609-086 later gave installs their own limiter (`throttle:product-install`, `routes/web.php:694 @HEAD`).
