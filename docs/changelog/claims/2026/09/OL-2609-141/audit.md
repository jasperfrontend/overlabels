- **F2** claim mislocated - C21 names `SavedChatPresetController` as listing looks `where product`; that controller has no list door (`SavedChatPresetController.php @41598c47`, methods store/apply/overwrite/rename/destroy/own/validateName/resolve); the scoped listing is `ProductDesigner::savedPresets()` at `ProductDesigner.php:340-341 @41598c47`, read by `ProductController::design()` line 487.
- **F3** scope - `tests/Feature/UserChatPresetTest.php` lost three tests the Surface line ("repointed; one new test") does not mention: "answers 404 for another product and for an account with no install", "requires a login" and "goes with the account when the account is deleted" (`@959d24b7:202-225`), and `git grep` at @41598c47 finds none of the three assertions elsewhere (a `saved-presets` store with no install, an unauthenticated store, a `forceDelete` cascade); 11 -> 9 tests. Either restore them or record the removal.
- **F4** scope - `tests/Feature/ProductChatDesignerTest.php` went 18 -> 16: besides the groups test the Surface names, "offers only layouts and backgrounds the overlay styles" and "declares the same vocabularies on the chat document itself..." (`@959d24b7`) were merged into "declares layouts and backgrounds the overlay styles, on the document itself" (`@41598c47:85`); undisclosed.
- **F5** scope - `resources/js/pages/products/design.vue @41598c47` also changes copy the Surface line does not cover: the page title `"Design your chat"` -> `` `Design ${product.name}` `` and the save-look blurb "Saves the skin, font, colors, layout and lifetime..." -> "Saves every knob below as it is right now..." (diff lines 261-271).
- **F6** contradiction with the record - `CLAUDE.md:772-773 @HEAD` (Chat Emote Bubbles subsection, not touched by this diff) still reads "no designer (the chat designer is hard-wired to `ChatPresets`; bubbles settings live on the Controls tab)", while the same commit deletes `ChatPresets` and gives Bubbles a designer; the rewritten subsection at `CLAUDE.md:793-829 @HEAD` says the opposite. Correct the Bubbles line.
